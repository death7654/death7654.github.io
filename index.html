ONE GAME_STATE · ONE PHYSICS LOOP · ONE WEBSOCKET ENDPOINT

Every device — both laptop displays, both phone controllers — connects to the same process over a single WebSocket. There's exactly one ball, one pair of paddles, one score. Nothing is duplicated per device, so the two halves never drift out of sync.

Scream to grow your paddle

03 · MIC INPUT
SCREAM POWER · GROWS YOUR PADDLE

Either controller can enable the mic. Volume feeds straight into the game loop as a live input — the louder you scream, the bigger your paddle gets on the shared court, right up until you stop and it settles back down.

Enable Scream Power is opt-in per player, so a quiet room and a loud one can still play each other fairly.
